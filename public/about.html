About PCL

PCL exists to give local indoor football a proper stage: real competition, real records, real recognition. From the pitch at N1 Padel to the stories that follow the players.

Our Story

Built in Polokwane, # pcl Thing.

PCL — the Polokwane Champions League — started as a simple idea: give local indoor football teams a serious, well-run competition they can be proud of.

For years, tournament football in the region ran on paper, WhatsApp groups and instinct. Results were lost, stats were forgotten, and fans had nowhere to follow the action. We built PCL to change that.

Today, PCL is a digitally managed football tournament — every team, player, fixture, goal and stat recorded and published. Teams register online, referees are assigned properly, standings are calculated automatically, and fans can finally follow the game they love.

But we're not done. PCL is becoming a proper sporting brand — a platform that connects players, fans, sponsors and eventually scouts.

Competitive integrity
Every result, every stat, every standing — recorded properly and calculated consistently. What happens on the pitch is what shows on the table.
Professional standards
Fixtures published early, referees assigned, results accurate. The small operational things that make a tournament feel real.
Community & growth
Players, fans, families, sponsors — everyone has a place in the PCL ecosystem. The more people who engage, the bigger the platform grows.

The Venue

Home of PCL — N1 Padel, Polokwane.

N1 Padel

PCL matches are played at N1 Padel — one of Polokwane's best indoor sporting facilities. The venue gives players a proper surface, fans a proper match-day experience, and the tournament a professional home.

Indoor pitches Professional-grade playing surface, ready for competitive football.
Kitchen & bar Hot dogs, shawarmas, coffee, ice cream — match-day food all day long.
Match-day atmosphere Fans, families, music, and full seating around the pitches.
Match days Saturdays and Sundays throughout the season. Fixtures published in advance.
How PCL Works

From registration to the trophy — four steps.

Register
Teams register online, submit their squad and player details. Administrators review and approve.
Schedule
Fixtures generated, referees assigned, and match days published at least two days in advance.
Play
Matches are played at N1 Padel. Every goal, card and event is recorded by the match official.
Publish
Results, standings and stats update automatically. Fans, players and sponsors see everything online.
